# D/s Reward + Punishment System
The goal is **playful structure and motivation**, not making every missed task feel heavy.
The system should feel like:
> “oh nooo 😭 I got a consequence”
not:
> “I now owe an impossible amount and the whole thing stresses me out.”
## Core Rules
- Keep **rewards more common than punishments**.
- Use **tiers**, so small things do not trigger huge consequences.
- Do not let punishment debt stack indefinitely.
- Allow **substitution** if a punishment does not feel right that day.
- Keep some higher-intensity things as **specialty options**, not automatic escalation.
- Normal love, affection, sex, and reassurance stay baseline relationship things; “reward” versions should be about **special intentionality**, not earning access to Taylor.
- Sickness, overwhelm, vacation, or genuine low-functioning days should have a **pause/reset mode**.

# System Rules
## 1. No Infinite Debt
A bad week should not create six punishments waiting in a queue.
## 2. Cap Outstanding Demerits
Once Jackie hits the cap, the system resets or converts into one consequence.
## 3. Intensity Does Not Scale Automatically
Three missed tasks should not automatically mean “three times harsher.”
## 4. Substitution Is Always Allowed
Same tier, different consequence if the original one feels wrong that day.
## 5. Pause Mode Exists
Examples:
- sick
- overloaded
- vacation
- bad executive-function day
- anything where punishment would feel punitive instead of playful
## 6. Higher-Intensity Punishments Need Fresh Agreement
Especially anything:
- restraint-heavy
- psychologically intense
- electrical
## 7. Rewards Should Outnumber Punishments
The system is meant to reinforce functioning, not build a disciplinary bureaucracy.
## 8. Keep Normal Affection Out of the Economy
Cuddles, reassurance, love, sex, touch, and emotional availability are not things Jackie should have to earn.
A **special version** of them can still be a reward.
## 9. Make Punishment Clearable
One consequence should resolve the issue.
Avoid leaving a lingering feeling of:
> “I’m still bad.”
## 10. Debrief the Stronger Ones
Especially immersive or psychological punishments.
Useful check-in:
> did this feel corrective, playful, too much, just right, or weird?

## “I have been disciplined”
- impact

## “take care of me”
- massage
- bath
- hair brushing
## “give me focused attention”
- games
- movie
- outing
- hobby time
## “make me feel especially wanted”
- scene rewards
- praise

That suggests a strong overall design principle:
> **Punishment is often about surrender and discipline, while reward is often about receiving care, pleasure, and focused attention.**
